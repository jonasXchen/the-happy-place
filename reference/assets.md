# Catalogue and imagery references

Retrieved on 14 October 2026 from [The Happy Place](https://www.thehappyplace.sg/). These are public business assets used to prepare the replacement website requested by the owner. Review publication rights and business terms before production launch.

`catalogue.json` contains 12 reference products with displayed SGD base prices, option labels, dimensions, estimated backorder lead times, paraphrased descriptions, local image paths, and source links. All entries require owner review. No current stock counts, variant price adjustments, purchasable option combinations, or SKUs were verified. Source option labels are reference values; they are not a validated Cartesian product of purchasable variants.

All 12 source listings were marked backorder when inspected. Do not advertise these products as ready to ship without an inventory update. Seed inventory counts or ready-to-ship examples used elsewhere in the app must be explicitly demo data. The source prices include GST; variant prices may differ from the displayed base price.

## Business information for review

The current site footer lists THP Studio, 12 Tampines North Drive 4, #07-04, Tampines Stellar, Singapore 529435; Tuesday to Sunday, 11am–6pm by appointment; Hello@thehappyplace.sg. Verify the address and hours with the owner before production use.

Source delivery text advertises standard courier above S$300 with exceptions for travertine and solid wood dining/coffee tables. It also lists S$150 delivery, installation and handling for dining tables, consoles and sideboards. These are category-level reference policies, not approved price rules for the replacement site. The source also states backorders are non-refundable, includes possible delay language, and mixes processing windows with arrival estimates. Keep policies in draft until reviewed.

The Celio source description refers to both a wood veneer finish and solid wood construction. The Eliesse description also mixes full sintered-stone wording with wood substrate construction and an unusual 1.8mm thickness statement. The Ismeralda description mentions geode while its materials section states Turkish travertine. Seed descriptions avoid these contradictory details and unverified durability, environmental, allergen, or pet-safety claims.
